Our Goals

Each goal we set brings us closer to giving children the life they deserve.

  • To reduce the number of vulnerable children living on the streets.
  • To improve access to education and healthcare for orphans.
  • To build a sustainable, loving support system around each child.
  • To prepare children for independent, confident adult lives.
  • To restore confidence and self-worth in children who have faced trauma.
  • To promote child rights and protect every child from abuse and neglect.
  • To reunite children with safe, supportive family members when possible.

About Us
At Adetola Homes, we are more than just an orphanage — we are a family. Founded with compassion and a deep sense of purpose, our home exists to care for orphaned, abandoned, and vulnerable children who deserve love, safety, and a second chance at life. Many of the children who come to us have faced extreme hardship — from losing parents to living in unsafe conditions. We welcome them with open arms, providing a stable environment where they can begin to heal, learn, and grow. Our team of caregivers, educators, counselors, and volunteers work together to meet each child's physical, emotional, and educational needs. We believe in nurturing the whole child — offering not just food and shelter, but also mentorship, creativity, spiritual growth (if applicable), and guidance for the future. We also engage with the wider community through outreach programs, advocacy, and partnerships to ensure no child is left behind. Whether it's reuniting children with safe family members, enrolling them in school, or simply celebrating their birthdays — every little moment counts. At our core, we believe that *every child is valuable* and capable of greatness. With love, support, and opportunity, we strive to help each one discover their potential and walk confidently into a better future.

Child Rights & Protection
Every child deserves to grow up in a safe, loving environment, free from abuse, neglect, and exploitation. We uphold children’s rights by ensuring their safety, protecting them from harm, helping them access legal identity and documentation, and involving them in decisions that affect their lives.

Our caregivers and staff are trained in child safeguarding, and we work closely with local authorities to defend and protect vulnerable children.


Right to Education & Health
Education and health are not luxuries, they are rights. We are committed to providing every child with quality education, access to healthcare, and proper nutrition.

From paying school fees to offering daily meals and organizing medical checkups, we make sure our children grow up informed, healthy, strong and ready to build a brighter future.
